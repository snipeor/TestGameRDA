Kingdom Rampart

Protect your keep from the nightly hordes! Move your monarch along the kingdom road and build watchtowers to fend off enemies. Survive as many waves as you can.

  • Tap ◀ / ▶ or use A / D or arrow keys to move.
  • Tap Build to place a tower at your position (costs 50 gold).
  • Tap Repair to restore a damaged tower (costs 40 gold).
  • Defeat enemies to earn more gold.
  • If enemies reach the keep, you lose a heart!
